About Us

Roofers Local 58 is a proud affiliate of the United Union of Roofers, Waterproofers, and Allied Workers, dedicated to representing skilled tradespeople throughout Colorado and portions of Wyoming. Our members specialize in commercial and industrial roofing, waterproofing, and other essential building trades that keep structures secure and communities thriving.

With a long-standing commitment to worker advocacy, we strive to ensure that all roofers receive the fair pay, benefits, training, and workplace protections they deserve. From negotiating strong collective bargaining agreements to fighting for safer job sites, our mission is to elevate the roofing trade for current and future generations.

Whether you're an experienced tradesperson or someone just starting out, Local 58 provides access to top-tier apprenticeship training, solid career pathways, and a union brotherhood that has your back on and off the job site.

Apprenticeship Program

We offer a 3-year apprenticeship program with competitive wages, healthcare coverage, retirement benefits, and raises every 6 months based on attendance, classroom participation, and monthly reports.

Apprenticeship includes OSHA 10 Construction Safety training and access to high-demand jobs—especially for those eligible to work on military bases and correctional facilities.

If you're ready to start a career with strong pay, benefits, and job security, apply to our apprenticeship today.
